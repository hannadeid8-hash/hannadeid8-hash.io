Nutrition and adjustment rules
  • Maintenance is roughly 2,800–2,900 kcal. Confirm it with 1–2 weeks of tracking.
  • Cut target: 2,350–2,400 kcal. Protein 190 g, fat 65–75 g, carbs fill the rest (mostly around training).
  • Aim to lose 0.5–1 lb per week, judged by the weekly average of daily weigh-ins.
  • Stalled under 0.5 lb/week for 2 weeks: cut 150–200 kcal from carbs or add 2k daily steps. Change one thing at a time.
  • Main lifts down more than 5% for 2+ weeks: you're losing too fast. Add 100–200 kcal back.
  • Week 10 is a diet break at maintenance (about 2,800 kcal).
  • Sleep 7–9 hours. Creatine 5 g/day.
  • After week 20, reverse diet: add 100–150 kcal per week until you're back at maintenance.
